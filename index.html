Frequently Asked Questions

We're Here to Answer Your Questions

Find answers to common questions about our caregivers, booking process, payments, and home nursing services.

1

What happens if my assigned caregiver becomes unavailable?

+

We maintain a backup caregiver network. If your assigned caregiver becomes unavailable due to illness or emergency, we will arrange a qualified replacement as quickly as possible and notify you immediately.

2

How does the payment process work?

+

A booking confirmation payment may be required depending on the selected service. The remaining balance can be completed according to your service plan through our secure payment system.

3

Are all caregivers verified and trained?

+

Yes. Every caregiver goes through identity verification, background checks, reference validation, and professional screening before being approved on our platform.

4

Can I change or cancel my booking?

+

Yes. You can request modifications or cancellations through your account dashboard or by contacting our support team.

5

What home care services do you provide?

+

We provide elderly care, patient care, post-surgery assistance, companionship, medication reminders, mobility support, and personalized home nursing services.

6

Can I request the same caregiver again?

+

Yes. If the caregiver is available, we will do our best to assign the same caregiver for future bookings to maintain continuity of care.

7

How do I communicate with my caregiver?

+

Once your booking is confirmed, you will receive caregiver details and can communicate regarding schedules, care preferences, and service updates.

8

Is emergency support available?

+

For medical emergencies, contact emergency services immediately. Our support team is available to assist with caregiver coordination and service-related concerns.
